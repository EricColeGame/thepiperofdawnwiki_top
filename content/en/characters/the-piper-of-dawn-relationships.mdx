## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I romance multiple companions in The Piper of Dawn relationships?
While you can flirt with several companions during Acts I and II, **The Piper of Dawn relationships** enforce a romance lock-in point toward the middle of Act III. Attempting to pursue multiple exclusive partners past this juncture will prompt a confrontation scene, forcing you to choose one companion or risk significant affinity penalties with both.

### What happens if a companion's affinity drops to zero?
If a character's affinity falls to zero or lower, they will enter a "Disillusioned" state. In this condition, they will refuse to join combat rotations, decline to converse in camp, and may permanently abandon your party or join opposing factions during key narrative confrontations.

### Are there missable loyalty quests in the game?
Yes. Every companion loyalty quest must be initiated and completed before progressing past the designated Point of No Return at the conclusion of Act II. Advancing the main questline past this milestone without resolving these missions will cause the associated characters to remain locked at their current affinity tier for the rest of the campaign.

### Do dialogue choices affect combat performance directly?
Yes. Deepening your bond unlocks Tier 2 through Tier 5 combat synergies, granting passive defense boosts, faster ultimate charge rates, and powerful tag-team abilities. Conversely, maintaining low affinity prevents access to these combat bonuses, making endgame encounters notably more difficult.
