### Act I: The Whispering Meadows and the Sunken Pipes

Your journey begins on the dew-covered slopes of the Whispering Meadows. Your initial objective is to retrieve the Reed Pipe from the sunken stone altar.

1.  Head eastward from the starting grove until you spot three upright basalt monoliths.
2.  Interact with the fallen wind chime to recover your introductory tuning reed.
3.  Align the wind vanes facing west, matching the direction of the morning breeze.
4.  Play the low alto vibration note while standing on the center stone plate to drain the canal water.
5.  Cross the dried riverbed and enter the Sunken Pipe chamber.

Within the chamber, you must rotate three circular stone dials to allow air pressure to flow into the primary organ. Set the left dial to two turns clockwise, the central dial untouched, and the right dial to one turn counterclockwise. Once air rushes through the primary chamber pipe, the gate unlocks, granting access to the highlands.

The Sunken Crypt presents some of the game's most challenging environmental logic. According to player experience logs, many adventurers stall here due to water level adjustments resetting acoustic echoes.

Keep this sequence in mind:

1.  Locate the central drainage lever behind the weeping maiden statue.
2.  Do not lower the water immediately; swim across to the eastern alcove to secure the **Sunken Resonant Key**.
3.  Return to the platform and pull the lever halfway to create a shallow reflection basin.
4.  Stand in the basin and play the treble melody. The sound wave will bounce off the shallow water surface, triggering all three wall receptors simultaneously.
5.  Ascend the spiral staircase before the automatic hydraulic pumps refill the chamber.
