## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the most crucial The Piper of Dawn resources to stockpile early?
During early progression, prioritize Common Shards, Sylvan Bark, and Resilient Resin. These three components handle all initial equipment repairs, entry-level weapon sharpening, and basic flask expansion. Having a healthy supply avoids mid-game detours when upgrade requirements climb.

### How does the in-game clock affect node respawns?
Certain premium nodes, particularly Dawnstone and Celestial Shards, only activate during the brief dawn transition in the world cycle. Standard flora and common minerals respawn every 15 to 20 real-time minutes, but dawn-exclusive nodes remain dormant until the next morning phase triggers.

### Should I sell surplus materials to local merchants?
It is generally inadvisable to sell raw crafting materials for basic currency. Low-tier reagents can often be transmuted into higher-grade components at advanced workshops later in your journey. Sell manufactured trinkets or duplicate equipment instead of raw materials.

### Can I respec materials spent on weapon upgrades?
While you cannot directly reclaim raw ores or herbs spent on basic gear tiers, late-game salvaging mechanics allow you to recover a portion of rare catalysts when dismantling fully upgraded equipment. Plan your builds carefully to avoid wasting high-end infusions.
