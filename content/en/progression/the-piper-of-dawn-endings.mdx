## Step-by-Step Walkthrough for Every Major Ending

### 1. Sovereign Dawn (The True/Purification Ending)

The Sovereign Dawn outcome represents the canonical "best" resolution, wherein the ancient curse over the land is lifted, and dawn breaks across the valley for the first time in centuries.

*   **Step 1:** In Chapters 1 through 3, resolve all town distress quests without using Discord melodies. Always favor restoration over brute-force solutions.
*   **Step 2:** Locate the four Lost Hymn Verses scattered across the Whispering Woods, Sunless Belfry, Sunken Parish, and Cloister Ruins.
*   **Step 3:** When encountering the Piper at the Hollow Spire, reject his bargain. Do not equip the Obsidian Mouthpiece at any point.
*   **Step 4:** During the Chapter 5 climax, play the *Hymn of the First Light* on the Consecrated Flute while standing in the center ring.
*   **Step 5:** Spare the weakened manifestation of the Piper to trigger the dawn cinematic sequence.

### 2. The Hollow Tune (The Corrupted Ending)

For players exploring darker storylines, The Hollow Tune shows the tragic consequences of succumbing to the dark melodies.

*   **Step 1:** Consistently choose aggressive and self-serving dialogue choices throughout the campaign.
*   **Step 2:** Absorb the resonance of at least three regional spirits rather than cleansing them.
*   **Step 3:** Accept the Piper's pact in Chapter 4, equipping the Obsidian Mouthpiece to permanently replace your standard embouchure.
*   **Step 4:** Defeat the Guardian of Dawn using only Discord spells and dark cadences.
*   **Step 5:** Play the *Dirge of the Void* at the central altar to seal the world in perpetual mist, taking the mantle of the new Piper.

### 3. Song of the Wanderer (The Neutral Exile Ending)

This path represents rejection of both divine duty and demonic corruption. The protagonist turns their back on the endless struggle, walking the misty roads alone.

*   **Step 1:** Maintain a balanced alignment score, keeping Harmony and Discord within 15 points of each other.
*   **Step 2:** Cleanse two regional shrines while leaving two corrupted.
*   **Step 3:** When presented with the choice at the altar of dawn, refuse to offer either the Purified or Corrupted Flute.
*   **Step 4:** Choose the dialogue option: *"Neither light nor shadow belongs in human hands."*
*   **Step 5:** Exit the chamber through the Western Archway without interacting with the central relic.

## Frequently Asked Questions About The Piper of Dawn Endings

### How many endings does The Piper of Dawn have in total?
The game features five distinct endings: Sovereign Dawn (True Harmony), The Hollow Tune (Discord/Corrupted), Song of the Wanderer (Neutral Balance), Silent Cadence (Low Resonance/Renunciation), and Midnight Requiem (Secret Cosmic). Each ending features distinct cinematic cutscenes and concludes the protagonist's narrative arc differently.

### Can I unlock multiple The Piper of Dawn endings in a single playthrough?
Yes, provided you create a dedicated manual save file right before entering the Grand Sanctum at the conclusion of Chapter 4. However, because routes like Sovereign Dawn and The Hollow Tune require conflicting Harmony and Discord thresholds built up over the entire campaign, you cannot jump between polar extremes on a single save file without utilizing New Game Plus.

### What is the canonical conclusion among the available paths?
While community consensus and thematic framing treat Sovereign Dawn as the traditional "True Ending," the developers have noted in progression logs that both Sovereign Dawn and Midnight Requiem provide complete contextual closure regarding the nature of the curse and the Piper’s ancient identity.

### Does game difficulty alter which ending you receive?
No. Difficulty settings only modify enemy health, puzzle hint frequency, and combat timing windows. All narrative branches and requirements for **The Piper of Dawn endings** remain identical whether playing on Story, Normal, or Nightmare difficulty.
